How the learning works

A positive reply is an interested reply or a question, a prospect you marked replied or won, or a paying client. Unsubscribes and "no thanks" don't count.

Something is called working or not working only once it has {{ r.min_n }} emails behind it and is at least {{ (r.sure * 100)|int }}% likely to beat, or trail, everything else. When many things are compared at once the bar is raised, so ten targets don't produce a lucky "winner" by chance. Until then it's "too early", even if the early numbers look good. Each rate starts from your overall reply rate ({{ '%.1f'|format(f.base * 100) }}%), so one lucky reply can't make a winner.

The agents use the same numbers when they choose. Most of the time they pick what looks best and sometimes they try something unproven, so a weak early read gets corrected. Changes to settings, like retiring a target or raising the score cutoff, go through the Strategist and wait for your OK unless it's on Autopilot.
